Galpi Privacy Policy

Operator: FAN BURSTER Studio · Effective: October 2, 2026
App: Galpi — Screenshot Organizer · com.fanburster.galpi

Galpi is an Android tool for organizing and finding screenshots you select or permit it to access. This policy covers local processing, optional transfers you choose, SDK diagnostics and customer support.

1. Data on your device

Galpi stores permitted images and file metadata, recognized text, folders, tags, titles, notes, review state, reminders and preferences on your device. Archiving or cropping can create private image copies. Semantic search creates a local search index. Galpi does not require an account or login.

2. Permissions

Photo access is used to find, read and organize screenshots. Depending on Android version you may allow all photos, selected photos or use the system picker. Notification permission is used for reminders you enable. You can change permissions in Android settings; declining can limit related features. Changes to shared originals, such as trashing or restoring, follow Android's required approval flow.

3. Local recognition and SDK diagnostics

Google ML Kit recognizes text on your device. Google states that input images and recognition outputs are not sent to its servers. Galpi does not include your photos, recognized text, notes or search queries in advertising requests or send them to its own analytics server.

ML Kit may send Google device model/manufacturer/OS, app package/version, per-installation identifiers, latency, errors, feature events and input format/resolution/size metrics for diagnostics, analytics and abuse prevention. These metrics are distinct from your image content and recognized sentences. Google states that these metrics are transmitted over HTTPS. Google's policies govern its processing and retention.

  • ML Kit data disclosure

4. Optional backup, sharing and sync

Backups can contain image copies, recognized text, folders, tags, notes and preferences depending on the selected scope. A storage provider you choose in the system file picker handles files under its own policies. Sharing passes selected data to the app you choose.

Remote sync is off by default. If you configure an HTTPS endpoint and credentials and enable sync, backups containing photos and metadata are exchanged with that server. Connection settings are protected locally using Android Keystore. Transfers use HTTPS, but backup files themselves are not guaranteed to be encrypted. Choose a trusted destination and review its access, retention and deletion practices. Disabling sync or uninstalling Galpi does not remove copies already stored elsewhere.

5. Optional search model download

If you choose to download the semantic search model, public model files are retrieved from Hugging Face. Its hosting service may process IP addresses and normal connection metadata. Photos, recognized text and queries are not included in the download request. Search computations run locally. Hugging Face Privacy Policy

6. Advertising

Google Mobile Ads and consent-management SDKs are included. The initial 0.1.0 release configuration has no production advertising IDs and does not request ads. Versions with ads enabled may show ads subject to applicable consent and choices. Google's advertising SDK may process approximate location inferred from IP addresses, interactions, diagnostics and identifiers such as advertising ID or app set ID for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Galpi does not provide photos, recognized text, notes or queries to the ad SDK. We update this policy and Play disclosures when that configuration changes. Google Mobile Ads disclosure

7. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you manage or delete it. The app's data settings let you reset organization metadata or erase private archived copies. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling removes app-private data. Gallery originals, shared files and external backups require separate deletion at their destinations. Provider-held diagnostics follow that provider's policy and are not data the Galpi operator can directly erase.

8. Support and requests

If you email us, we process your email address, message and attachments to investigate and respond. Do not send sensitive photos or passwords. Support records are retained as necessary to address the request, subject to deletion requests and legal retention obligations. Contact furster.official@gmail.com for access, correction or deletion requests. We cannot remotely access or delete photos or notes stored only on your device.

9. Children, international services and changes

Galpi is a general personal-organization tool and is not primarily designed for children. Google, GitHub, Hugging Face and destinations you choose may process data internationally under their own policies. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and adds no analytics tool or advertising cookies. Policy updates will be posted here with a revised effective date.
